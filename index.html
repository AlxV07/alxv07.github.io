Hey, I'm Alex! I'm a student researcher and software engineer. I'm exploring CS/AI and Classics.

Ancient language, philosophy, and debate are the foundations of modern thought and science. I believe the timeless wisdom found in studying Classics - independent rational judgement, effective rhetoric, and a humanistic worldview - is just as important as technical expertise.

With the rise of AI, genuine human reasoning and understanding becomes ever the more critical to ensure progress is grounded in truth. My work explores how NeSy and explainable AI can ensure technology is honest, transparent, and accessible, to benefit humanity.
