1st Place at Dallas Regional Science & Engineering Fair

02/28/2026

Today at the Dallas Regional Science & Engineering Fair (DRSEF) hosted at Fair Park, I placed first in my category of "Software Design / Systems Software" and advanced to the State-level competition. I presented my project "MobilePipe: iPhones as Parallel Compute Accelerators for Local Machine Learning", a full experiment analysis framework built upon my NeurIPS proof-of-concept research paper "iOS as Acceleration". It was a great experience presenting my research and meeting other competitors, and I'm looking forward to competing at the State fair (TXSEF) hosted at A&M University next month!
